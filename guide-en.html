Getting started with Mix

Mix is a free music player. Your music lives in your own Telegram channel, and Mix plays it on your phone and computer: with smooth crossfades, offline, with covers, lyrics and smart mixes.

    1Install Mix

    iPhone

    1. Open the Mix link in Safari (Safari specifically, not inside Telegram or Chrome).
    2. Tap “Share” (square with an up arrow) → “Add to Home Screen” → “Add”.
    3. Open Mix from the Home Screen icon — that way it works like a normal app and keeps playing with the screen off.

    Android

    1. Open the link in Chrome → “⋮” menu → “Install app” (or “Add to Home screen”).

    Computer

    1. Just open the link in a browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox). In Chrome and Edge there’s an “Install” button in the address bar — Mix will open in its own window.

    To switch the language: Mix → Settings → Language → English.

    Desktop shortcut (Windows)

    1. Chrome or Edge (best option): open Mix → the “Install” icon on the right of the address bar (a monitor with an arrow) → “Install”. If there’s no icon: ⋮ menu → “Cast, save and share” → “Install page as app” (in Edge: ⋯ → “Apps” → “Install this site as an app”). A Mix shortcut appears on the desktop and in the Start menu, and Mix opens in its own window.
    2. Pin to the taskbar: open Mix from the shortcut → right-click its taskbar icon → “Pin to taskbar”.
    3. Firefox can’t install sites as apps. Make a shortcut manually: right-click an empty spot on the desktop → “New” → “Shortcut” → paste https://traaarik1.github.io/mix-player/ → “Next” → name it Mix → “Finish”.
    ImportantOpen Mix from one place only — one shortcut or one tab. If Mix is open in two windows at once, Telegram may reset the login.

    Phone shortcut

    • iPhone: Safari → “Share” (square with an arrow) → “Add to Home Screen” → “Add”. Always open Mix from that icon.
    • Android: Chrome → ⋮ menu → “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.

    MacBook / Mac

    1. As a separate app. In Safari (macOS Sonoma and later): open Mix → File menu → “Add to Dock”. In Chrome: the “Install” icon on the right of the address bar (or ⋮ menu → “Cast, save and share” → “Install”). Mix appears in the Dock and Launchpad and opens in its own window.
    2. Launch at login: System Settings → General → Login Items → “+” → choose Mix in Applications (for Chrome — in “Chrome Apps”).
    3. Control without the window. While Mix plays, it shows up in Control Center (top-right icon → “Now Playing”): pause, seek, next track. The F7 F8 F9 keys and AirPods buttons work too.
    4. Mini player on top of windows — the closest thing to a widget. In Mix tap the mini player button at the bottom right (next to the queue). A small window with the cover and buttons stays on top of all apps. Works in Chrome and Edge; not available in Safari.
    What about a real desktop widget?macOS only allows widgets for regular App Store apps, not websites. So instead: the mini player on top of windows and “Now Playing” in Control Center.

    2Create a music channel

    1. In Telegram tap “New message” (pencil) → “New Channel”.
    2. Name it anything, e.g. “My music”. Make it private so only you can see it.
    3. Done. Anything you send or forward there as audio will appear in Mix.
    Already have a channel or chat with music?That works too, even “Saved Messages”. You don’t have to create a new channel.

    3Get Telegram keys

    Mix connects to Telegram directly from your device, without third-party servers. For that, Telegram gives everyone free keys.

    1. Open my.telegram.org and log in with your phone number. The code arrives in Telegram itself (from the official “Telegram” account).
    2. Tap “API development tools”.
    3. Fill in the form: App title — Mix, Short name — mixplayer (any Latin word), Platform — Web. Leave the rest. Tap “Create application”.
    4. You’ll see App api_id (a number) and App api_hash (a long string). Paste them into Mix in the next step.
    Never send your keys or Telegram codes to anyoneEnter them only in Mix itself. Mix keeps them on your device and doesn’t send them anywhere.
    The site shows “ERROR” when creating the app

    It happens: turn off VPN and ad blockers, try from a computer or another browser, change the Short name to another word. Sometimes retrying a few minutes later helps.

    4Connect Telegram to Mix

    1. In Mix tap “Connect Telegram”.
    2. Paste api_id and api_hash.
    3. Enter your phone number and the code Telegram sends (and your cloud password if you have one).
    4. Pick your music channel from the list.
    5. Mix starts loading tracks. On a computer it downloads the whole channel; on a phone — what you play.

    5Adding music

    • Forward to the channel any audio from Telegram: chats, channels, music bots.
    • Files from your computer: drag mp3/m4a/flac into the Mix window or tap “+ Files”. Mix offers to send them to the channel so they appear on your phone too.
    • “Wishlist”: in the Wave, search, albums and artist pages there’s a plus — the song goes to your wishlist. The “Find” button opens your music bot in Telegram with the title already filled in. Forward the found track to your channel — Mix replaces the preview with the full version.
    Titles and coversMix recognizes tracks by sound, fixes messy titles, adds original covers and sorts tracks into albums and artists. For the best recognition add a free AcoustID key: Settings → AcoustID key.

    6Move playlists from Spotify, Apple Music & co

    1. In Mix: menu → “Import playlist”.
    2. Paste a Deezer playlist link (works everywhere), Spotify or Apple Music (work in Mix on a computer launched via the desktop shortcut). From any other service paste a list of songs as text: one per line, “Artist - Title”.
    3. Mix builds a playlist from what you already have and puts the rest into “Wishlist” — easy to add with the “Find” button.

    Spotify and Apple Music don’t share the song files, so Mix moves the list, and you add the music to your channel.

    7Computer and phone together

    • Log in to Mix with the same Telegram on all devices — likes, playlists, history and fixed titles become shared. Sync works through “Saved Messages” in your Telegram (a service file mix-sync.json appears there, don’t delete it).
    • The fastest way to connect a phone: in Mix on the computer tap “Connect phone” and point the phone camera at the QR code.

    8What Mix can do

    • Wave — an endless mix of your music and similar new tracks that learns from what you play and skip.
    • Smooth crossfades like a DJ, even volume, equalizer.
    • Works offline: downloaded tracks always play.
    • Artists and albums: discography, popular tracks, similar artists, what’s missing from an album.
    • New releases from your artists, mixes (top of the month, haven’t heard in a while…), stats — what and how much you listened to.
    • Synced lyrics and finding a song by a line.
    • “What’s playing?” — recognizes a song around you via the microphone.
    • Radio from a track, queue, swipes on the phone, lock-screen and keyboard controls.

    9FAQ

    Is it safe? Who sees my music?

    Mix is a page that runs on your device. It connects directly to Telegram with your keys; everything is stored in your browser and your Telegram. Other Mix users (and the author of Mix) can’t see your data.

    Does Mix send anything about me?

    Only anonymous counters: “Mix installed”, “Telegram connected”, “came via an invite”. No name, number, tracks or cookies — just numbers so the author can see how many people use Mix.

    How much does it cost?

    Nothing. Mix is free, so is Telegram.

    Why does a track take a while to start the first time?

    If a track isn’t downloaded yet, Mix gets it from Telegram. After the first time it stays on the device and starts instantly, even offline.

    A track was recognized wrong

    “⋯” on the track → “Edit title” (search or type manually) or “Restore original title”. After a manual fix Mix never touches that track again.

    On iPhone music stops when the screen turns off

    Open Mix from the Home Screen icon, not from Safari. Then it plays in the background and works from the lock screen.

    Everything disappeared after refreshing the page

    Wait a few seconds — Mix reloads itself if an update arrived only partly. Data is stored on the device and doesn’t go anywhere. Don’t clear site data in browser settings.
